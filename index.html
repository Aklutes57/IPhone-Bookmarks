        Help

        What is this app?

        This is a simple home screen for the websites you use most. It takes the bookmarks you already saved in Chrome and lays them out like the app icons on your phone, so your favourite pages are one tap away. Nothing is shared with anyone, and everything stays on this device.

        Step 1: Export your bookmarks from your computer

        Your bookmarks live in Chrome on your computer, so we start there.

        1. Open Chrome on your computer.
        2. In the address bar at the top, type chrome://bookmarks and press Enter.
        3. Near the top right of that page, click the small three-dot menu (⋮).
        4. Choose Export bookmarks.
        5. Chrome saves a single file ending in .html. Pick somewhere easy to find, like your Desktop.

        This also works with bookmark files exported from Safari, Firefox, or Edge — the steps are the same, just use that browser's Export Bookmarks option.

        Step 2: Get the file onto your phone

        Now send that file to the phone you are reading this on. Any way you like works:

        1. Email the file to yourself and open the email on your phone, or
        2. Upload it to Google Drive, Dropbox, or iCloud and open it there, or
        3. Use AirDrop or a USB cable if that is easier for you.

        You just need to be able to reach the file from your phone. You do not have to open it yet.

        Step 3: Import into this app

        1. Tap the three-dot menu (⋮) in the top right of this app.
        2. Choose Import bookmarks.
        3. Tap the box that says to choose a file, and pick the .html file you moved over.
        4. Give this set of bookmarks a name, like Personal or Work.
        5. Tap Import. Your bookmarks appear as tiles right away.

        You can repeat this any time you want to add another Chrome account or refresh an existing one.

        Add this app to your home screen

        For the real app feeling, add this page to your home screen so it opens full screen with its own icon.

        On Android (Chrome): tap the three-dot menu in Chrome, then choose Add to Home screen or Install app.

        On iPhone (Safari): tap the Share button (the square with an arrow going up), scroll down, and choose Add to Home Screen.

        What about my passwords?

        This app never sees, asks for, or stores any of your passwords. It only knows the name and web address of each bookmark. When you tap a tile, the page opens in your normal browser, and Chrome fills in your logins exactly the way it always does. Your accounts stay as safe as they were before.

        Frequently used

        To show the Frequently used row, the app counts how often you tap each tile. That count is stored only on this phone, is never sent anywhere, and disappears if you clear the app's data.

        Settings

        Tap the three-dot menu (⋮) at the top right and choose Settings to change how the app looks and behaves.

        Appearance. Choose System, Light, or Dark. System follows whatever your phone is already set to. Light and Dark keep the app in that look no matter what your phone does.

        Open links in Chrome. This choice appears on iPhone only. Turn it on and your tiles open in the Chrome app instead of Safari. If you tap a tile and nothing happens, Chrome may not be installed on your phone, so the app will offer to open the site here instead.

        Back up your bookmarks

        Settings also lets you save a copy of everything in the app, so you can put it back later or move it to another phone.

        Save backup. In Settings, tap Save backup. On iPhone the Share sheet appears, so choose Save to Files to keep the file on your phone, or send it to yourself with AirDrop or email.

        Restore backup. Tap Restore backup and pick the file you saved. This replaces everything on this phone with the backup, exactly as it was when you saved it.

        It is worth keeping a backup somewhere safe, especially on iPhone, where stored data can be cleared when an app has not been opened for a while.

        Rearranging tiles

        You can put your tiles in whatever order you like.

        1. Tap the three-dot menu (⋮) and choose Edit.
        2. Press and hold a tile for about half a second, then drag it where you want and let go.

        Folders always stay together in alphabetical order, so you are arranging the site tiles around them. This works when you have a single account selected, not the All view. If you later re-import an account to refresh it, that account's custom tile order and any tile colours you picked are reset.

        Troubleshooting

        Some tiles show a letter instead of a logo. That is completely normal. When a website does not offer a clear icon, we show the first letter of its name on a coloured tile instead. The bookmark still works the same way.

        My bookmarks disappeared on iPhone. iPhones sometimes clear stored data for web apps that have not been opened in a while. If that happens, just import your bookmarks file again using the steps above and everything comes back.

        The file would not import. Make sure you chose the .html file that Chrome exported, not a screenshot or a different document. If in doubt, export a fresh copy from your computer and try again.
